📋 How to connect to phpMyAdmin
  1. Import bnis_database.sql into phpMyAdmin — creates bnis_db with Grade 11 & 12 SHS data
  2. Edit config.php — set your MySQL username & password
  3. Copy all files into C:\xampp\htdocs\bnis\ (XAMPP) or your WAMP folder
  4. Open http://localhost/bnis/AdminDashboard.html
⚠ Database Error

Cannot connect. Check your PHP server and config.php.
