Junior High School (JHS)

Our Junior High School program provides a strong foundation for students in Grades 7-10, preparing them for Senior High School and beyond.

7

Grade 7

Foundation year focusing on core subjects and adjustment to high school life.

8

Grade 8

Building on fundamentals with advanced concepts in all subject areas.

9

Grade 9

Specialized tracks introduction and career exploration begins.

10

Grade 10

Preparation for Senior High School with career guidance and track selection.

JHS Core Subjects

  • 📚 English
  • 📚 Mathematics
  • 📚 Science
  • 📚 Filipino
  • 📚 Social Studies (Araling Panlipunan)
  • 📚 Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE)
  • 📚 Music, Arts, Physical Education, and Health (MAPEH)
  • 📚 Values Education (Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao)

Senior High School (SHS) Strands

BNIS offers Academic and TVL strands such as STEM, HUMSS, ABM, ICT, and EIM (Electrical / Cookery) to prepare you for college or employment.

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics - Perfect for students interested in engineering, medicine, research, and technology.

Possible College Courses:

  • 🎓 Engineering (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer)
  • 🎓 Medicine and Health Sciences
  • 🎓 Information Technology & Computer Science
  • 🎓 Architecture
  • 🎓 Pharmacy
  • 🎓 Mathematics & Statistics
  • 🎓 Physics, Chemistry, Biology

Core Subjects:

Pre-Calculus Basic Calculus General Biology General Chemistry General Physics

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences - Ideal for students interested in law, education, communication, and social sciences.

Possible College Courses:

  • 🎓 Law
  • 🎓 Education (Elementary, Secondary, Special Education)
  • 🎓 Mass Communication & Journalism
  • 🎓 Psychology
  • 🎓 Political Science
  • 🎓 Social Work
  • 🎓 Philosophy
  • 🎓 Literature & Creative Writing

Core Subjects:

Creative Writing Disciplines & Ideas Philippine Politics World Religions

ABM

Accountancy, Business and Management - For future entrepreneurs, accountants, business leaders, and hospitality professionals.

Possible College Courses:

  • 🎓 Accountancy
  • 🎓 Business Administration & Management
  • 🎓 Marketing Management
  • 🎓 Tourism & Hospitality Management
  • 🎓 Economics

Core Subjects:

Business Math Organization & Management Principles of Marketing

ICT

Information and Communications Technology - For students interested in programming, networking, multimedia, and IT-related jobs.

Possible Careers & Courses:

  • 💼 IT Support Specialist / Technician
  • 💼 Web & Graphic Designer
  • 🎓 BS Information Technology
  • 🎓 BS Computer Science

Core Skills:

Computer Systems Servicing Programming Basics Multimedia Editing

EIM / Cookery

Electrical Installation & Maintenance and Cookery (TVL) - Hands-on training for students who want to gain technical skills for work or business.

Possible Careers & NC (TESDA):

  • 🔌 Electrician (EIM NC II)
  • 🍳 Cook / Kitchen Staff (Cookery NC II)
  • 🏠 Household / Building Maintenance

Core Skills:

Electrical Installation Safety Procedures Food Preparation
