March 8, 2026
Infrastructure

New School Library Wing Now Open

The newly constructed library annex, funded through the DPWH allocation, is now fully operational. It holds 3,000 new books and 20 computer stations with internet access.

February 25, 2026
Facility

School Canteen Renovation Completed

The main school canteen has been fully renovated to comply with DepEd Nutritional Food Standards. New stalls are now operational with healthier food options for students.

February 10, 2026
Technology

Campus-Wide Wi-Fi Installation Underway

Free Wi-Fi access points have been installed in 12 classrooms and the library. Installation in remaining areas of the campus is expected to be completed by March 30.

January 22, 2026
Environment

School Garden Program Expanded

As part of the DepEd School-Based Feeding Program, BNIS has expanded its vegetable garden to five additional plots. All Grade 8 and Grade 9 TLE students will participate in weekly garden maintenance.

January 15, 2026
Policy

Updated School Uniform Policy Effective Q3

Beginning the 3rd Quarter, students are reminded to follow the updated BNIS uniform policy. PE uniforms must only be worn on designated PE days. Complete uniform checklist is available at the Guidance Office.
